Our story

Two worlds,
one heart

Some love stories begin with a single glance across a room. Teshwin and Fanny's began somewhere between continents — a Malaysian soul and a French spirit finding in each other exactly what the other never knew they were searching for.

From their first conversation, there was a warmth that felt like coming home. Teshwin's quiet tenderness met Fanny's vibrant, open heart, and something undeniable was born. Through distance, through difference, through the gentle navigation of two cultures learning to dance together — they chose each other, again and again.

Today, their story lives in the Netherlands, where sambal meets baguette on the breakfast table, where Tamil love songs play beside French chansons, and where every day is proof that love does not need a single language to be perfectly understood.

On the 26th of February 2028, on the golden shores of Port Dickson, they invite you to witness the next beautiful chapter.

"Two cultures, one love — and an entire world made richer for it."

— Teshwin & Fanny

Attire

Dress code

We warmly invite our guests to celebrate in the spirit of the occasion. Traditional Indian and cultural attire is greatly encouraged and deeply appreciated — let's paint the shore in colour!

Traditional Indian attire

Sarees, lehengas, salwar kameez, kurtas, sherwanis, and dhotis are all welcome and celebrated. The brighter and more festive, the better.

Other cultural & formal wear

Kebaya, baju kurung, French or Western formal wear — all cultural dress is honoured and welcome. Smart casual is the minimum.

Colour palette

Rich jewel tones, vibrant reds, saffron, royal blue, emerald and gold are encouraged. Please avoid white and black as primary outfit colours.

Footwear note

The ceremony is held on the shorefront. Flat or wedge footwear is recommended for comfort on the sandy grounds.
